A groovebox that fits
in your pocket.

HeartBeat is a 16-track sequencer, sampler and synth studio for iPhone - built for hands-on pattern writing: parameter locks, trig conditions, live sampling and a board full of devices.

What's on board

Groovebox sequencing

Parameter locks on every knob, trig conditions, retrigs, micro-timing and six-voice chords - per step, per track. Sequencer →

A board full of devices

Six sampler modes - one-shot, repitch, warp, stretch, slice, grid - plus mono, poly, FM, wavetable and acid synths, a drum synthesizer and a plucked string. Devices →

Piano roll editing

A full note editor over the same trigs: marquee selection, group drag, per-voice gates and its own undo stack. Piano roll →

Live sampling

Record from mic, input or resample the master - then slice it, warp it to tempo, or play it chromatically. Sampling →

LFOs, lanes & morphs

Tempo-synced LFOs on any knob, recordable automation lanes, and A/B scene morphing you can ride live. Modulation →

A full FX board

Per-track drive, chorus and ring-mod; shared delay and reverb; DJ FX, EQ, compression and a master chain. FX & mixer →

MIDI everything

Learn any control from any controller, route every track to hardware, and lock the groove with Ableton Link. MIDI & Link →

Projects that travel

Self-contained project folders you can see in the Files app, sound presets, and a factory project to start from. Projects →
