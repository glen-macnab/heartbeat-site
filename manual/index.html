HeartBeat Manual

HeartBeat is a 16-track groovebox for iPhone: a hardware-style step sequencer driving samplers, synths and external hardware, with performance tools layered on top. This manual covers all of it.

The Perform screen

The header names the loaded project - color square and title on the left - with a menu on the right switching between PERFORM, PROJECTS and SETTINGS. Perform is where you'll live. Top to bottom:

BandWhat it is
TransportPlay, record, Q, FILL, tempo, the pattern button, and the MIDI-learn button with its activity LEDs. →
Top carouselSwipeable pages: VIZ (the device visualizer), GFX (delay & reverb), MIX (mixer), MFX (DJ FX), EQ, MSTR (dynamics), RTE (compressor routing). →
Param areaTRG (the step editor) plus the selected device's knob pages. Latch a step and every knob writes p-locks. →
Step grid16 steps per page, up to 128 per track, with the clipboard row (COPY, PASTE, piano roll, UNDO, REDO) above. →
Track pads16 track pads, re-purposed by the mode row: MUTE, PLAY, MORPH, GEN, KEYS. →

The color language

  • One hue per track, carried through every surface - pads, steps, faders, meters, waveforms.
  • Amber always means "set on the step / armed": p-locks, lock notches, queued patterns, page loops, FILL.
  • Cyan is reserved for MIDI learn.
  • Violet is morphs; red is record.

Five minutes to a beat

  1. Pick a track: tap pad 1. A fresh sampler track meets an empty pool and seeds the factory drum kit automatically.
  2. Place trigs: tap steps 1, 5, 9, 13. Press play.
  3. Shape the sound: swipe the param area to FLTR or AMP and turn knobs; watch the visualizer follow.
  4. Lock a step: long-hold step 5, turn FREQ. Amber arc, amber notch: that step now differs. Unlatch and keep going.
  5. Add movement: hold FILL for your fill steps, push a step's PROB below 100, or put an LFO on the filter. From there: devices, the piano roll, morphs…
